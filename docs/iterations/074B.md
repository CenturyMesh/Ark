## Compatibility and verification

074B source semantics are conditional: new region arrangements, body retention
and batch Boolean recipes require explicit version gating rather than being
silently interpreted by an older runtime. Existing recipes keep their behavior.
The physical SQLite container remains independent of authored source semantics.

Record before/after runtime hashes, identical inputs/resource settings, failed
attempts, exact/cold reference comparisons and human-control captures. Final
installer, upgrade/rollback and retained user-state checks remain separate gates.

  Complex assembly mechanisms keep their conservative solver; memoized bodies
  still require current request/disk/restore authentication.
- R3: [typed transactions, immutable Undo and image validation](../TRANSACTION_STORAGE_074B.md).
  No history pruning or missing-data shortcut was introduced.
- R4/G3/G4/G5: [advanced surface and preview contracts](../SURFACES_ADVANCED_074B.md).
  Matching uses compatible positive homogeneous bases and rectangular trims;
  networks require compatible crossing gauges. Closed is not a claim of global
  periodic derivatives. General arbitrary trimmed matching remains unsupported.
- R6: [matched Boolean measurements](../BOOLEAN_PERFORMANCE_074B.md).
- G1/G2/G5/G6/G7: [native solid contracts](../SOLID_AUTHORING_074B.md) and

  Point anchors and body selection reject boundary/ambiguous matches. Current
  digest-bound handles are not advertised as persistent IDs. Thin offsets reject
  topology loss, collapse and self-intersection instead of discarding material.

The extrusion editor now exposes intersecting cells, separate-feature versus
one-multibody output, native thin offsets and explicit join modes. Depth-only
editing preserves authored region-point expressions. Target unions use one
explicit batch, including the bridge-between-separate-bodies case. Variable
fillets expose an origin edge and 2-16 fraction/radius station pairs with
expressions, checked preview and atomic Apply. The rebuild report separates
preparation from publication and explains changed/reused feature dependencies.

## Verification and delivery

Focused native and human controls passed before the final sweep. The first broad
run found two real regressions: historical unbound reference-group metadata was
being rewritten, and first-use SHA-provider destruction registration could block
the background memory queue during shutdown. Both were fixed without changing
the original oracles/timeouts. The same shutdown child now exits and persists
its expected episode; historical named groups keep their metadata while genuine
split lineage uses current references.

Additional checks exposed and corrected parametric-loft reference identity
(two arcs must not be bound by equal endpoints), native thin offset handling of
multiple boundaries and their orientation, and adaptive area integration for
rational surfaces. Actual source geometry and acceptance tolerances were kept;
initial fixtures whose sweep sign did not match their analytic intent remain
recorded, with the original C0 geometry additionally tested against an independent
line/arc area integral.

Final core validation passed **206/206 native/script suites** and **29/29
affected Studio flows**. The final protocol-guide recheck additionally covers
the updated onboard descriptions. Native caches, persistence, failure/rollback,
source59 gates and the original modeling/memory regressions are included.
Actual normal/compact controls, geometry previews and the rebuild report were
visually inspected. Source commit and installer hashes follow the separate
distribution checks. No user drawing, conversation or credential is a
fixture or package input; no manufacturing or universal geometry qualification
is implied by these local tests.

Delivery-byte rechecks are retained in `out/performance-074b-delivery` and
`out/performance-074b/storage-boolean-delivery.json`; they supersede earlier
checkpoints for the final timings above while retaining their evidence.
