# 052D — isolated part agents

Technical version: **0.9.53**. Milestones: **A.03/B.04/D.01/D.02/D.03**.
Implementation: **Verificado no escopo** locally. The broader milestones remain
**Parcial**; only the synthetic detail task below has live-model evidence.

Focused Studio teams delegate existing part containers into real private
documents. Designers receive local context and read-only dependencies, retain
parametric expressions and submit frozen results. Lead review and atomic
integration require the original source revision, provenance and reviewed child
digest. No global prompt/image is automatically sent to part designers.

The [workflow contract](../PART_AGENTS_052D.md) describes capabilities, current
container boundaries and the lack of automatic rebase or persistent task queues.
Parallel remains optional with its previous workflow.

The benchmark adds actual scoped delegation, optional explicit designer models,
per-role measurements and a synthetic reused-part fixture. A separate neutral
mode gives Single and PartFocused identical user task text and records its hash;
the lead may decide to perform a small edit directly. Actual delegation remains
separately identified. Authentication is explicit, including the connected ARK
ChatGPT account without importing another application's credentials.

## Live pilot: forced delegation

2026-10-22, `gpt-6.1-sol`, `medium`, ARK ChatGPT-plan Responses route. Two
repetitions, alternating strategy order; the same synthetic baseline, model,
effort and geometric/persistence oracle. The task changes a reused part's depth
from 6 to 9 mm, preserves the `SharedWidth` expression and the other module.
PartFocused explicitly requires a private edit and lead integration.

| Strategy | Accepted | Reported total tokens, both attempts | Median attempt time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Single | 2/2 | 157,654 | 38.91 s |
| Forced PartFocused | 1/2 | at least 759,815 | 165.37 s |

Both delegated attempts produced valid, integrated geometry that survived
save/reopen. The second was conservatively rejected as a completed outcome after
an HTTP 504 during lead completion. Its failed request has missing usage; it is
not zero-cost. Tool failures were 0/0 for Single and 3/5 for delegated attempts.
The first successful delegated trial alone used 384,259 tokens versus 85,234 for
its Single control. This small edit does not establish savings from delegation.

## Live pilot: neutral decision

The separate neutral preset ran two repetitions with the same model, effort,
baseline, criteria and identical user-task hash in both strategies:
`9bac307ed2532094274d99f6d6af89c3ed44b08a7b92c7f470414953b528c2d9`.
The lead was free to choose delegation; no private-edit requirement was added.

| Strategy | Accepted and reopened | Mean tokens per attempt | Mean attempt time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Single | 2/2 | 72,630.5 | 35.19 s |
| Neutral PartFocused | 2/2 | 244,286 | 96.94 s |

All four attempts had complete provider usage and zero tool failures. In both
team attempts, the engineer edited directly and used the reviewer; the designer
remained inactive. This measures the current team's coordination/review overhead,
not context savings from private part editing. The neutral team used **3.36x**
the reported tokens and **2.75x** the elapsed time of Single on this small edit.
Neither the neutral trial nor the forced trial supports a savings claim.
