## Implemented scope

- [Inertial dynamics](../INERTIAL_SIMULATION_056C.md): revision-bound derived
  rigid-body studies with hardware GPU integration/contact/joint solving and an
  explicit CPU reference. Native CAD mass/inertia remains authoritative;
  supported collision geometry is a validated convex display mesh. The
  [Studio panel](../INERTIAL_UI_056C.md) exposes settings, occurrence loads,
  background Run/Cancel and derived playback/reset through Inspect > Simulation.
- [Viewport](../VIEWPORT_056C.md): actual Off/FXAA/high-quality FXAA controls,
  screen-space contact shadows, bilateral AO and shared GPU meshes with packed
  normals. Exact geometry, source positions and picking identities are retained.
- [Project storage and commands](../PROJECT_SCALE_COMMANDS_056C.md): integrated
  derived compression from PR #35 with transactional compaction and stronger
  manifest checks; searchable parameterized command discovery and exact-tool
  activation through existing onboarding and preview contracts.
- [BOM and public research](../BOM_RESEARCH_056C.md): supplier search-to-page
  handoff, bounded product evidence, actionable per-part recovery and direct
  exposure of sourcing tools in BOM workflows.

The base includes PR #39 and its #36–#38 ancestry. The technical compression

## Evidence and limits

Windows Release, LLVM-MinGW 23.1.3, patched OCCT 8.0.1, RTX 4070 Ti. Component
observations and initial failed attempts are in the linked contracts.

- The integrated build completed. Of 165 non-Studio CTest suites, 162 passed
  initially. Three discovery failures identified an omitted drawing-authorization
  rule in command onboarding and two obsolete tool-count assertions. The rule
  was restored; assertions now inspect actual tools, schemas and permissions.
  All 11 affected/relevant suites then passed, including the three failures,
  command discovery, assistant/tool layers, BOM, simulation and release evidence.
- The native inertial suite passes 60 checks across CPU reference and hardware
  GPU, including analytic mechanics, momentum, peripheral support, inertia,
  hinge drift, mesh reuse, invalid scopes and unchanged CAD. Fixed poses remain
  exact rather than round-tripping through float32 display state.
- Actual Studio CPU/GPU studies passed at 1440x900 and 1180x800, including native
  mass, occurrence loads, scheduling, playback and reset without authoring changes.
  The minimum-size process exited 0. Captures of controls, evidence and model
  display were inspected. This is local synthetic-fixture integration evidence.
- Nine of ten selected Studio regressions passed initially: scene cache,
  inertial controls, viewport quality, selection glow/picking, render, action
  icons, baked motions and exploded views. The additional background exact-read
  test found a real hit-priority conflict: the floating dock intercepted the
  visible Cancel button in a short viewport. Its correction/recheck is pending.
