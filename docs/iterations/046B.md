# 046B - profile regions, motion clearance and background exact reads

Runtime 0.9.47 and iteration identity come from `release.json`. Phase **B**
(CAD authoring) is the principal focus. Milestones **A.04, B.02, B.03, C.04**
and **D.04** remain **Parcial**. The scopes below are **Verificado no escopo
local**: synthetic fixtures, the regression suite and local Studio captures on
one Windows machine. No dynamics, manufacturing, speed-up, design-quality or
cost claim is made.

Geometric authority, `expected_revision`, atomic publication and explicit
rejections are unchanged. Only an authored `native.regions` selection requires
document format **48**. Documents without it keep their earlier required
version, and motion clearance and background reads persist nothing.

## Profile regions (B.02, B.03)

Before 046B a sketch with several closed loops was rejected by every solid
path: native extrude ("Profile wire does not close"), the legacy mesh extrude
("Nested sketch loops require separate cut features") and `extrude_surface` and
sweep sections ("profile entities are not connected in supplied order"). A
plate with holes needed one cut per hole.

Now several non-touching closed loops form one profile by even-odd containment:
depth 0 is an outer boundary, depth 1 a hole and depth 2 an island.

- **Consumers:** `native_solid` extrude (distance, symmetric, reversed, up to
  face), the legacy extrude and its exact promotion, `extrude_surface` and
  `revolve_surface` with `solid:1`. A closed inner loop revolves into an
  enclosed void.
- **Hollow sweep:** an outer wire with inner loops sweeps as a hollow section.
  All loops share one rigid placement on the path, the result must conserve
  volume, and the inner sweeps are cut with an exact Boolean.
- **Checks:** the OCCT worker builds loops from exact edges, measures that loops
  do not touch, and checks every region face against outer-minus-holes area.
  Crossing, touching, branched, zero-area and self-intersecting loops and open
  chains without a selection reject, naming the entities.
- **One solid per feature:** several regions (disjoint outer loops or an
  island) reject unless `native.regions` selects one by an outer-loop entity
  or an expression point seed. Hole walls keep entity-derived `side.<entity>`
  names.
- **Legacy identity:** single-loop sketches send byte-identical requests. Their
  B-rep, reference and mesh digests equal the 045A build with Windows MSVC and
  OCCT 8.0.1, the toolchain they were recorded on. The Linux build serializes
  one legacy case to different bytes, so there the test checks only that legacy
  cases keep the single-loop path.

## Motion clearance over baked motions (C.04, D.04)

`motion_info{name,clearance}` checks a fresh bake read-only. A stale bake
rejects with `MOTION_BAKE_STALE`, and nothing is re-baked or saved.

- **Measurement:** frames are re-solved in double precision and measured with
  exact B-rep distance in bounded native batches (`collision_frames`, up to 128
  poses per request) behind a box prefilter.
- **Certification:** a frame interval is certified when the smaller end
  clearance exceeds a conservative displacement bound. The bound is proven for
  rigid-side joint trees and assumed for closed loops and transmissions, which
  are never `continuous_certified`. Undecided intervals are bisected through
  the bake solver.
- **Binding:** results bind to the revision, `source_digest` and
  `graph_digest`. Directly mated, same-joint and rigid-cluster pairs are skipped
  by default and counted.
- **Studio:** the Baked Motions panel adds Check clearance, a threshold, red,
  amber and hatched timeline markers and a worst-frame link. The check runs as
  a background exact job (below).

## Exact reads without blocking Studio (A.04)

Before 046B every tool request, including long exact reads, ran on the Studio
UI thread.

Live MCP, Assistant and team requests for allow-listed read-only exact tools
now run on a private session copied at the revision reached after every earlier
queued request:

- **Allow-listed tools:** `native_inspect`, `geometry_query`,
  `reference_inspect`, `assembly_info`, `collision_inspect`, `physics_inspect`,
  `mesh_inspect`, `motion_info`, `step_inspect`, and a `query_batch` made only of
  them.
- **Writes** stay on the serialized UI queue.
- **Labels:** results carry `observed_revision` and an `exact_job` label. A
  later change keeps the label instead of rebasing the read.
- **Cancel:** a chip above the viewport dock shows the running check with
  **Cancel**, while orbit, zoom and selection stay live. Cancelling stops the
  isolated native worker within 50 ms.
- **Limits:** inside a job, one native request may run 120 s (30 s elsewhere)
  and the whole job 600 s, with `RESOURCE_LIMIT` wording.
- **Internal API:** `submitExactJob046B` lets Studio code run the same jobs.
  The Baked Motions Check clearance action uses it.

## Not established

- **Profile regions:**
  - there is no Studio region picker;
  - islands and disjoint outer loops need their own feature or a selection;
  - loft, surface fill, split-line and hole-cutter profiles do not take
    regions, nor do varying-section or law sweeps;
  - the legacy mesh revolve keeps one loop;
  - byte identity of legacy results on Linux was not compared against a Linux
    045A build.
- **Motion clearance:**
  - baked motion is ideal kinematics; there are no dynamics, contact mechanics,
    deformation or tolerance stacks, and no penetration depth;
  - closed loops and transmissions are assumed, not proven;
  - joint excursions between the sampled points of fast function nodes and
    couplings are not proven;
  - the certified floor is conservative for long links.
- **Background reads:**
  - writes, full recompute and Studio dialogs (mass properties, measurement)
    keep the busy renderer;
  - `export_step`, drawing and manufacturing inspections are not allowed in
    jobs;
  - cancel acts only at native requests;
  - only one synthetic fixture was measured. The earlier real 30 s spline
    clearance and three-size p95 were not reproduced.
